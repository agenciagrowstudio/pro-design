Frequently asked questions

Clear answers,
before we begin.

Still have a question? Call (857) 333-9140 and talk it through with us.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Tell us a little about your project and we will discuss the best next step. Estimates are always free, with no obligation.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Uxbridge, Massachusetts and the surrounding communities. Ask us about your town when you call.

Do you work on interior and exterior projects?

Yes. PRODESIGN provides professional interior and exterior painting for residential spaces, plus deck washing, finish carpentry and drywall taping.

Do you install drywall?

No. We provide drywall taping and finishing only, including mudding, sanding and paint-ready preparation. We do not hang or install drywall.
